# Import and Data setup

Use the pages in this section to set up your depots and users and to import your deliveries into TourSolver.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **TourSolver** with a user profile that has access to the **Configuration** page.
* Prepare your delivery data, for example in an Excel file. You can download a sample file to see the expected format.

## Feature overview

* **Create a Depot**: Create, import, and export the depots that you use for scheduling and resource allocation.
* **Create and Modify a User**: Create new users and update the details, roles, or schedules of existing users.
* **Import of Deliveries**: Upload your deliveries, map your spreadsheet columns, and review the geocoding results.
* **Modify Address of a Delivery**: Update the address of a delivery and recalculate the route.
* **Manage Agencies**: Share resources between agencies, and move users and deliverers to another agency.
