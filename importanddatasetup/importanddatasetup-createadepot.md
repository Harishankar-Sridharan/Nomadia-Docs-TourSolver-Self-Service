# Create a Depot

Create, import, and export the depots (warehouses, service centers, or hubs) that you use for scheduling and resource allocation.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **TourSolver**.
* Open **Configuration** and select **Manage Depots** from the **Actions** menu.

## Feature overview

* **General section**: Sets the name, the address, and the constraints of the depot.
* **Planning section**: Sets the opening days, the opening time, and the start and end times of the depot.
* **Quantity section**: Sets the initial quantity and the available space.
* **Import and Export**: Manages several depots at once.

### Depot details

| Section | Field or setting | Purpose |
| ------- | ---------------- | ------- |
| General | **Name** | Identifying label for the depot. |
| General | **Address** | Physical location of the depot. |
| General | **Constraints** | Rules that govern how the depot operates, such as Assign Resources, Explore Resources, or Availability. |
| Planning | Opening days, opening time, start time, end time | Defines when the depot is operational. You can also edit multiple opening days from this section. |
| Quantity | Initial quantity, available space | Defines the capacity and the inventory available at the location. |

## Open the depots page

1. Open the **TourSolver** web application.
2. Click **Configuration**.

3. From the **Actions** menu, click **Manage Depots**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (22).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Add a depot

1. From the **Actions** menu, click **Add**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (23).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. In the **General** section, enter the **Name** and the **Address**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (25).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click **Planning**.
4. Adjust the **opening days** and the **opening time**.
5. Define the **start** and **end** time.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (26).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. To add or edit quantity details, click the **plus** symbol at the bottom right corner.
7. Edit the **initial quantity**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (27).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

8. Click **Add**.

### Result

The depot is created successfully.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (28).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Export and import depots

Use the import and export functions to manage several depots at once.

1. On the main depot management screen, click the **Actions** menu.
2. Click **Export** to download the file.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (29).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click **Import**.
4. Select **Browse Excel** to choose a file, or drag and drop your file into the upload area.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (30).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Tips

{% hint style="info" %}
**Edit several days at once**: You can edit multiple opening days from the **Planning** section to save time when you set complex schedules.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Use the export as a template**: Export your depots first to get a template with the correct format. Then use it to import new depots in bulk.
{% endhint %}
