# Customer Communication

Use the pages in this section to configure the emails and the SMS messages that you send to your customers.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **TourSolver**.
* Have a sender email address that you can open, because you must validate it.
* Know that the outgoing SMS service is a paid service.

## Feature overview

* **General Configuration**: Enable the outgoing email and SMS services and validate your sender email.
* **Time Window to Communicate**: Define the timeframe that you communicate to a customer for an upcoming visit.
* **Upcoming Arrival**: Send an ETA link so that the customer can track the live location of the deliverer.
* **Next Visits**: Notify your upcoming customers that you are on your way.
* **End of Visit**: Send a PDF proof of delivery once the delivery is completed.
* **Visit Cancellation**: Mark a visit as cancelled and let the customer select a new date.

## Tips

{% hint style="warning" %}
**SMS length**: Each SMS message is limited to 160 characters. A longer message is split into two chargeable messages.
{% endhint %}
