# View agendas

This page explains how to use the **Agendas** view to see when your deliverers are available.

## Before you begin

* Open a simulation. See [Simulation](simulation.md).

## Steps

1. Go to **Simulation** > **Details**.
2. Click the **Details** drop-down arrow, and then click **Agendas**.
3. Read the timeline. Each row is a deliverer, for example **Driver1** or **John**, and the hours of the day are at the top.
4. To change the day, click the arrows on each side of the date, or click the calendar icon.
5. To see the meaning of the colors, click the information icon next to the panel title.

![The Agendas view with the availability of the deliverers on a timeline](../.gitbook/assets/TS_Simulation_DetailsAgendas_001.jpg)

## Result

The colors show **Available** (green), **Assigned** (blue) and **Unavailable** (gray).

![The legend of the Agendas colors](../.gitbook/assets/TS_Simulation_DetailsAgendas_002.jpg)
