# Unassign routes

This page explains how to remove the assignment of the selected routes.

## Before you begin

* Open a simulation. See [Simulation](simulation.md).

## Steps

1. Go to **Simulation** > **Routes**.
2. Select the check boxes of the routes you want to unassign.
3. Click the **Routes** drop-down arrow, and then click **Unassign**. You can also press **Alt+n**.

## Result

The deliveries of the selected routes are unassigned. You can check them in the **Planned** and **Assigned to** columns of the **Deliveries** panel.
