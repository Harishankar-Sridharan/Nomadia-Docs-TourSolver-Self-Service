# How secure and confidential is my data?

Understand how TourSolver keeps your business data secure and confidential.

## Feature overview

TourSolver is built around the [Microsoft Azure Cloud architecture](https://azure.microsoft.com/). Two characteristics of this platform protect your data and your service:

* **Multi-tenancy management**: Your business data remain secure, confidential, and impenetrable while stored in the application.
* **Elastic, optimized architecture (auto-extendable)**: It adapts to peaks in utilisation and to variable geographic coverage requirements, so service continuity is assured.

Together, these conditions offer a seamless experience in the Cloud with TourSolver.
