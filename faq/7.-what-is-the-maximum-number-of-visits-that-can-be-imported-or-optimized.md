# 7. What is the maximum number of visits that can be imported or optimized?

* In the **TourSolver Self-service trial version**, you can import and optimize up to **250 visits**
* With an **active subscription**, the limit ranges from **2,000 to 4,000 visits**, depending on the specific constraints set for your simulation.\
  Certain constraints—such as multiple opening days or visit frequency—can significantly increase computation time and may reduce the maximum number of visits allowed.
