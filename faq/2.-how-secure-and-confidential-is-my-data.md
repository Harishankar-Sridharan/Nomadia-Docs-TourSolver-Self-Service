# 2. How secure and confidential is my data?

TourSolver is built around the [**Microsoft Azure Cloud architecture**](https://azure.microsoft.com/).

This means that your business data remain secure, confidential, and impenetrable while stored in the application thanks to this platform’s ‘multi-tenancy’ management system. Service continuity is assured by its ‘elastic’, optimized architecture (auto-extendable) that adapts to satisfy peaks in utilisation, and variable geographic coverage requirements. These conditions together offer a seamless experience in the Cloud with TourSolver
