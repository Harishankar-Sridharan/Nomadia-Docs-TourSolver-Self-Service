# How do I reactivate a licence of the TourSolver legacy version?

Reactivate the licence of the TourSolver desktop version (MapPoint) with the activation wizard, and find out how to move to TourSolver Cloud.

## Before you begin

* Have the new licence key that Nomadia sent you.
* Have the Datapack key associated with your licence, if your version uses map data.

## Feature overview

* **Activation wizard**: Activates or updates the licence of the TourSolver desktop version.
* **Update a licence**: The option of the activation wizard to enter a new licence key.
* **Datapack key**: The key of the map data associated with your licence.
* **TourSolver Cloud**: The web version of TourSolver, which replaces the legacy version.

## Update the licence

1. Open the TourSolver desktop version.
2. Open the activation wizard.
3. Select **Update a licence**.
4. Enter the licence key that Nomadia sent you, and then confirm.
5. If the wizard asks for it, enter the associated Datapack key.
6. Keep the new activation key that the wizard shows. You need it if you reinstall TourSolver.

### Result

Your licence is active again, and you can use the TourSolver desktop version.

## Move to TourSolver Cloud

The maintenance of the TourSolver legacy version has ended. To keep receiving updates and support, move to TourSolver Cloud:

1. Contact your account manager to get a TourSolver Cloud subscription.
2. Log in to TourSolver Cloud. See [Log in to TourSolver](../getting-started/log-in-to-toursolver.md).

## Tips

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Keep your keys**: Save the licence key, the activation key, and the Datapack key in a safe place. Support needs them if you change your computer.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**No new licence key?**: Contact your account manager. Support cannot create a licence key.
{% endhint %}
