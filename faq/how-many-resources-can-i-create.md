# How Many Resources Can I Create?

Find out how many resources your TourSolver account allows.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **TourSolver** with your administrator account to see your subscription details.

## Feature overview

| Account type | Number of resources |
| ------------ | ------------------- |
| **TourSolver Self-service trial version** | Up to **5 resources** to create and optimize. |
| **Active subscription** | Depends on your subscription plan. |

## Check your subscription

1. Sign in to **TourSolver** with your administrator account.
2. Go to **Profile** > **My Subscription**.
