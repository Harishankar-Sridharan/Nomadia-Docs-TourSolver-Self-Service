# What is the Sent Email List?

Monitor the emails that the system sends to your customers.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **TourSolver**.
* Set up the outgoing email service. See [General Configuration](../customer-communication/general-configuration.md).

## Feature overview

The **Sent Email List** is a monitoring section where you can view all the emails sent by the system. It includes the emails triggered by these customer communication workflows:

* Time window to communicate
* Next Visits
* Upcoming arrival
* End of visit
* Visit cancellation

## Tips

{% hint style="info" %}
**Check the delivery status**: Use the Sent Email List to check the delivery status of your emails.
{% endhint %}
