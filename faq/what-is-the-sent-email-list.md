# What is the Sent Email List?

Monitor the emails that the system sends to your customers.

## Feature overview

The **Sent Email List** is a monitoring section where you can view all the emails sent by the system, including those triggered by these customer communication workflows:

* Time window to communicate
* Next Visits
* Upcoming arrival
* End of visit
* Visit cancellation
