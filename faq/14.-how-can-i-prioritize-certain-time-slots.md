# How can I prioritize certain time slots?

Guarantee a delivery time slot for a customer, even when it makes the route longer.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **TourSolver**.
* Know the customers who need a specific delivery time, such as premium customers.

## Feature overview

Sometimes the customer experience takes priority over reducing kilometers or CO₂ emissions. For example, premium customers can require deliveries at specific times to ensure their satisfaction, even if it results in longer routes.

* **Fixed Slot constraint**: Locks the specified time slot for the customer, so that they receive their delivery at the desired time.

## Prioritize a time slot

1. Select the customer delivery that needs a priority time slot.
2. Use the **Fixed Slot** constraint to lock the time slot.

## Tips

{% hint style="info" %}
**Flexibility**: Using Fixed Slot reduces the flexibility of the optimization algorithm, because it limits the route permutations. However, it guarantees that premium customers are served at the right time.
{% endhint %}
