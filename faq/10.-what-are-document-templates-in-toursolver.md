# What are Document Templates in TourSolver?

Understand what document templates are and what they generate.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **TourSolver**.

## Feature overview

Document templates are predefined Word templates that TourSolver uses to automatically generate documents. For example:

* **End-of-visit report**: The document sent to the customer once the visit is completed.

## Tips

{% hint style="info" %}
**End-of-visit documents**: To send the end-of-visit report to your customers, see [End of Visit](../customer-communication/end-of-visit.md).
{% endhint %}
