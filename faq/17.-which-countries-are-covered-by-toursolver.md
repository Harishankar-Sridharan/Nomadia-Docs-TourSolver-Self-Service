# Which countries are covered by TourSolver?

Check which countries TourSolver covers and where the geographical sectorization is available.

## Before you begin

* Know the country or the countries in which your deliveries take place.

## Feature overview

As of **May 2, 2018 (version 5.0)**, TourSolver provides routing and optimization coverage across **approximately 230 countries**, representing **over 7.2 billion people**. With recent additions such as **Bangladesh, Bhutan, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, and Sri Lanka**, TourSolver covers almost the entire planet.

Coverage is organized by region, and some regions include **geographical sectorization**, available in the **Premium version**.

### Countries not covered

* **North Korea**
* **South Korea**
