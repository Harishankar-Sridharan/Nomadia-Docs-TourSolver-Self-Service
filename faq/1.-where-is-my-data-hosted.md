# 1. Where is my data hosted?

Where your data and the infrastructure required to use TourSolver are stored will depend on the scope of your geographic coverage, and so, ultimately, on which country you are operating in when performing route optimizations.&#x20;

For [all countries supported](https://www.toursolver.com/en/faq/toursolver-geographic-coverage) in TourSolver (with the exception of China), the hosting of the Cloud infrastructure and your data will be in Europe and Northern Ireland.

For China, a dedicated architecture is replicated on the platform assigned for this country and hosted in China East in Shanghai
