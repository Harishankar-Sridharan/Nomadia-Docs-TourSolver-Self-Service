# How long are my Route optimizations kept?

Find out how long TourSolver stores your saved route optimizations.

## Feature overview

Each route optimization performed in TourSolver is saved in a simulation. A simulation is stored for a duration that depends on its validation:

* A simulation that is **saved but not validated** is stored for **two months**.
* A simulation that is **saved and then validated** is stored for **a year (12 months)**.

## Validate a simulation

You can validate a simulation in one of two ways:

* From the [list of simulations](https://www.toursolver.com/en/faq/managing-simulations-toursolver) accessible from the TourSolver **Home** page.
* From the **optimization results** window on the **Optimization** page.
