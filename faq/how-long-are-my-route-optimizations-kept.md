# How long are my Route optimizations kept?

Find out how long TourSolver stores your saved route optimizations.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **TourSolver**.
* Run and save a route optimization. Each saved optimization is stored in a simulation.

## Feature overview

A simulation is stored for a duration that depends on its validation:

| Simulation status | Storage duration |
| ----------------- | ---------------- |
| **Saved but not validated** | Two months |
| **Saved and then validated** | A year (12 months) |

## Validate a simulation

You can validate a simulation in one of two ways:

* From the [list of simulations](https://www.toursolver.com/en/faq/managing-simulations-toursolver) accessible from the TourSolver **Home** page.
* From the **optimization results** window on the **Optimization** page.

## Tips

{% hint style="info" %}
**Validate what you keep**: Validate the simulations that you want to keep, so that they are stored for 12 months and not for two months.
{% endhint %}
