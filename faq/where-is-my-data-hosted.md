# Where is my data hosted?

Find out where TourSolver stores your data and the infrastructure that you need to use it.

## Before you begin

* Know the country in which you operate when you perform route optimizations. The hosting depends on it.

## Feature overview

* **Hosting location**: Your data and the infrastructure required to use TourSolver are stored according to the scope of your geographic coverage.
* **Supported countries**: For [all countries supported](https://www.toursolver.com/fr/faq/couverture-g%C3%A9ographique-de-toursolver) in TourSolver, the Cloud infrastructure and your data are hosted in Europe and Northern Ireland.
