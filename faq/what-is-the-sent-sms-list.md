# What is the Sent SMS List?

Monitor the SMS messages that the system sends to your customers.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **TourSolver**.
* Set up the outgoing SMS service. See [General Configuration](../customer-communication/general-configuration.md).

## Feature overview

The **Sent SMS List** shows all the SMS messages sent to customers. It includes the messages sent as part of these communication workflows:

* Time window to communicate
* Next Visits
* Upcoming arrival
* End of visit
* Visit cancellation

## Tips

{% hint style="info" %}
**Verify delivery and consumption**: Use the Sent SMS List to verify the SMS delivery and consumption.
{% endhint %}
