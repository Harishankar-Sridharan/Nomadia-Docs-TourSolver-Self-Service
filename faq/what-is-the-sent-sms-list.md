# What is the Sent SMS List?

Monitor the SMS messages that the system sends to your customers.

## Feature overview

The **Sent SMS List** shows all the SMS messages sent to customers as part of these communication workflows:

* Time window to communicate
* Next Visits
* Upcoming arrival
* End of visit
* Visit cancellation
