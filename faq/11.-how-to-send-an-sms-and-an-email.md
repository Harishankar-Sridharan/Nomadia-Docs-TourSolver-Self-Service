# How to send an SMS and an Email?

Set up and send SMS and email notifications to your customers.

## Before you begin

* Sign in to **TourSolver**.
* Make sure that the customer contact details are available:
  * For emails, the customer must have an **email address**.
  * For SMS, the customer must provide a valid **mobile phone number**. For best results, enter the number in **international format**, including the country code. It improves compatibility and ensures the system can reach the customer wherever they are.

## Feature overview

* **Email templates**: Customize the sender email address, the subject, the message content, and the links.
* **SMS templates**: Customize the sender name, the subject, the message content, and the links.
* **Customer Experience Pages**: Configure the URLs that are used inside the email or SMS templates.
* **Notification toggle**: Sends the configured SMS or email when routes are published or when specific events occur.
* **Sent lists**: Check the delivery status of the emails and the SMS messages.

## Set up the email templates

1. Go to **Configuration** > **Outgoing emails**.
2. Customize:
   * **Sender Email Address**
   * **Subject**
   * **Message content** (static text and dynamic fields) for **Time window to communicate**, **Next Visits**, **Upcoming arrival**, **End of visit**, and **Visit cancellation**
   * **Links** (ETA link, feedback link, and so on)

## Set up the SMS templates

1. Go to **Configuration** > **Outgoing SMS**.
2. Customize:
   * **Sender Name**
   * **Subject**
   * **Message content** (static text and dynamic fields) for **Time window to communicate**, **Next Visits**, **Upcoming arrival**, **End of visit**, and **Visit cancellation**
   * **Links** (ETA link, feedback link, and so on)

## Configure the notification templates

1. Go to **Configuration** > **Customer Experience Pages**.
2. Configure the URLs that are used inside the email or SMS templates. The links are:

| Link | What it does |
| ---- | ------------ |
| **Delivery Time Window Link** | Gives the customer the **scheduled delivery time window**. The customer can view the expected start and end times of their delivery slot. |
| **Delivery Tracking Link (ETA)** | Lets the customer **track the delivery in real time**. It shows the **estimated time of arrival (ETA)** and updates as the route progresses. |
| **End of Delivery Link** | Confirms that the delivery was **completed successfully**. The customer can view the final delivery details, such as the completion time and the status. |
| **Reschedule Link** | Lets the customer **reschedule their delivery**, by selecting a new time window or date. |

## Send the notifications

1. When routes are published, or when specific events occur, such as **time-window communication, arrival notifications, or end-of-visit updates**, enable the **notification toggle**.
2. TourSolver sends the configured SMS or email to the customer, so that they receive timely updates about their delivery or visit status.

## Check the delivery status

1. Use the **Sent email list** to view the email delivery status.
2. Use the **Sent SMS list** to verify the SMS delivery and consumption.

## Tips

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Notification toggle**: TourSolver sends nothing until the notification toggle is enabled.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**International format**: Enter phone numbers with the country code, so that the system can reach the customer.
{% endhint %}
