# 11. How to send an SMS and an Email?

* **Ensure customer contact details are available**
  * For emails: the customer must have an **Email address**.
  * For SMS: the customer must have a **Mobile phone number**.
  * The **Receive notifications** option must be enabled for the customer.
* **Configure your notification templates**\
  Go to **Configuration → Customer experience pages** and set up templates for the events you want to notify customers about, such as:
  * Time window communication
  * Upcoming arrival
  * End of visit
  * Visit cancellation
* **Set up Email templates**\
  Go to **Configuration → Outgoing emails** and customize:
  * Sender address
  * Subject
  * Message content (static text + dynamic fields)
  * Links (ETA link, feedback link, etc.)
* **Set up SMS templates**\
  Go to **Configuration → Outgoing SMS** and configure:
  * Sender name/number
  * SMS message text
  * Dynamic fields such as communicated time window, ETA, or feedback link
* **Send notifications automatically**\
  When routes are published or when specific events occur (time window communication, arrival notification, end-of-visit), TourSolver automatically sends the configured SMS or email to the customer.
* **Check the delivery status**
  * Use **Sent email list** to view email delivery status.
  * Use **Sent SMS list** to verify SMS delivery and consumption.
