# How to modify my subscription

Update your plan, your license quantity, or your payment details from the Subscription settings.

## Before you begin

* Have your TourSolver account login.

## Modify your subscription

1. Log in to your account.
2. Go to **Settings** > **Subscription / Billing**.
3. Click **Modify Subscription**.
4. Choose what you want to change:
   * Update the plan.
   * Increase or decrease the licenses, vehicles, or users.
   * Adjust the product quantity.
5. Review the new price and click **Confirm** to save the changes.
