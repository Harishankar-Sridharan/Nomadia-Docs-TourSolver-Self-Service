# Why do I have waiting time in my route plan?

Understand why the optimization algorithm adds waiting time, and how to reduce it.

## Feature overview

The optimization algorithm can introduce waiting time for these reasons:

1. The deliverer arrives at the customer location **before the opening hours**.
2. The deliverer reaches the depot **before it becomes operational**.

Customer and depot opening times are strict constraints with a high priority, so the algorithm must respect them.

## Tips

{% hint style="info" %}
**Use Automatic Start Time**: To reduce unnecessary waiting time, use the **Automatic Start Time** constraint. It adjusts the departure time so that the deliverer starts later and arrives at the customer or at the depot at the right time. Idle periods at unknown locations are minimized.
{% endhint %}
