# Getting-Started

Create your TourSolver account and learn what you can do with the platform.

## Before you begin

* Use a supported browser. See [Which Browsers and Systems Are Compatible with TourSolver?](faq/which-browsers-and-systems-are-compatible-with-toursolver.md).
* Have a valid email address to sign up. No installation is needed, because TourSolver is a fully cloud-based solution hosted on Microsoft Azure.

## Feature overview

* **Trial account**: Lets you test the platform immediately, with up to 5 drivers, up to 5 route optimizations per day, and up to 250 deliveries per optimization.
* **Import and Data setup**: Create depots and users, import your deliveries, and modify their addresses.
* **Resource and Vehicle Management**: Create and modify resources, and rename vehicle quantities.
* **Operations and Route Management**: Optimize your deliveries, export your routes, share a tour with the mobile app, and geolocate your delivery people.
* **Simulation**: Manage deliveries and routes, view the map, and analyze the results.
* **Customer Communication**: Configure the messages that you send to your customers.
* **FAQ**: Answers to frequently asked questions.
